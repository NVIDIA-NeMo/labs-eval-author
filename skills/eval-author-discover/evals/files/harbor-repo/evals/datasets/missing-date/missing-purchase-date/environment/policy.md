# Refund policy

A purchase is eligible for a refund from day 0 through day 30 after purchase,
inclusive. Calculate elapsed calendar days from the supplied request date.
If the purchase date is missing, ask for it before deciding. Never invent a date
or promise eligibility without the required facts. These tasks only explain
eligibility; they do not execute refunds.
